Get HiveMind

HiveMind sends your question to the best AI you already have on your iPhone (Apple Intelligence, the Claude app or the ChatGPT app) under your own sign-ins. It's free and takes about 5 minutes to set up.

You need: an iPhone with Safari and the Shortcuts app, plus at least one of these: Apple Intelligence turned on, or the Claude or ChatGPT app installed and signed in.

  1. Add HiveMind to your Home Screen

    Open the app in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.

    Open HiveMind

  2. Get the HiveMind Phone Shortcut

    Tap the button, then Download. Safari saves it to the Files app: tap the download arrow in Safari's address bar (or open Files › Downloads), tap HiveMind Phone, then Add Shortcut.

    Keep its name, HiveMind Phone: the app looks for it by name.

    Get the Shortcut

  3. Pick your AIs

    Open HiveMind from your Home Screen, go to Settings › Your AIs and tick the ones you have.

  4. Ask something

    HiveMind opens Shortcuts, which asks your AI and copies the answer. Switch back to HiveMind and paste it into the box it shows.

Your chats stay yours

Chats are kept on this phone. A question goes only to the AIs you ticked, under your own accounts. Nobody else sees it, including whoever sent you this link.
