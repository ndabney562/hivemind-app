# HiveMind (phone app)

A web app for your iPhone that answers with the AIs already on your phone: Apple Intelligence, the Claude app and
the ChatGPT app, under your own sign-ins. Nothing leaves your phone except to those apps.

1. Open this site in Safari on your iPhone, then Share › Add to Home Screen.
2. Build the **HiveMind Phone** Shortcut from the recipe linked in the app, so the app can reach those AIs.
3. In the app, Settings › Your AIs: tick the ones you have.
